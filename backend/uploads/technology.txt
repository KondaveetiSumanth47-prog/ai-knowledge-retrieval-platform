Technology Knowledge Base

Retrieval Augmented Generation, or RAG, is a technique that retrieves relevant information from a knowledge base before generating an answer.

ChromaDB is a vector database that can store embeddings and perform similarity search.

Embeddings are numerical representations of text that capture semantic meaning.

Vector Search finds information by comparing the similarity between embeddings.

A Large Language Model, or LLM, is an AI model that can understand and generate human-like text.
